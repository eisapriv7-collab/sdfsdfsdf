How it works

Ninety weighted votes combine 37 technical indicators, 30 binary/candlestick pattern detectors, 12 short-term composite setups, 10 independently implemented TradingView-inspired bar-close systems, and one custom Adaptive Regime Fusion strategy. The technical layer examines EMA ribbons, Ichimoku, Supertrend-style ATR direction, DMI, Aroon, regression, RSI, MACD, PPO, volatility bands, CCI, stochastic, Williams %R, Ultimate Oscillator, Elder Ray, Hull-MA impulse, Fisher transform, momentum, channels, support/resistance, Heikin-Ashi, pivots and the Awesome Oscillator. The pattern layer checks pin bars, engulfing and harami candles, inside/outside bars, doji rejection, marubozu, tweezers, three-candle drives, morning/evening stars, three-inside reversals, piercing/dark-cloud reversals, kickers, belt holds, spinning tops, rising/falling three methods, double tops/bottoms, liquidity sweeps, trend pullbacks, 1-2-3 reversals, three-line strikes, streaks, market structure, NR4 compression, fractal breaks and breakout retests.

The composite layer adds QQE-plus-EMA, WaveTrend-style exhaustion, Traders Dynamic Index, SSL Channel, Squeeze Momentum, Connors RSI, DeMarker, Chande Momentum, Relative Vigor, EMA-RSI pullbacks, Bollinger-RSI snapbacks, and triple-confirmed Ichimoku setups. The custom strategy first classifies the setup as trending, ranging, or transitional, then changes its mix of trend-following, momentum, mean-reversion, structure, volatility, and pattern confirmation. The TradingView-inspired layer adds UT Bot ATR trailing logic, Chandelier Exit, volatility-explosion confirmation, linear-regression candles, zero-lag LSMA, KAMA, Vortex, AlphaTrend-style filtering, HalfTrend-style structure, and a multi-period Donchian ribbon. Every strategy contributes to a weighted 90-vote ensemble vector. The probability is calibrated from recency-weighted historical setups resembling both the combined score and the complete vote profile.

Balanced calibration

The calibration removes the loaded history's overall UP/DOWN imbalance before evaluating a similar setup. This prevents a small historical class imbalance from making every output point in the same direction, while still allowing repeated DOWN or UP predictions when the actual indicators continue to support them.

Validation

The displayed paper metrics are produced chronologically. Each historical probability uses only setups that came before it. Ties are treated as refunds. Payout-adjusted returns use a fixed one-unit stake and never use Martingale.

OTC file mode

OTC mode runs the same 90-vote ensemble entirely on imported five-minute OHLC candles. Its prediction is for the five-minute candle immediately after the final completed row. Re-import an updated file at the next boundary for a fresh prediction.

Limits

Regular mode uses Deriv's public EUR/USD feed. OTC mode has no live broker connection and knows only the exact file you supply. The page does not know Quotex payout changes, settlement latency, future news, or orders that have not reached the broker. A different data source can close the same five-minute period at a different price.

What this mode does

It reads the 1-minute EUR/USD chart and predicts whether price will be higher or lower 5 minutes after the entry, which is a 5-minute binary trade. A new signal comes at every 1-minute close, so you get many more chances than waiting for 5-minute candles.

Top-down analysis, pro-trader style

1) Higher-timeframe bias: 15-min and 5-min trend, rebuilt from 1-min EMAs. 2) Market structure: swing highs and lows, HH/HL vs LH/LL. 3) Liquidity: sweeps of the 20-bar high/low (stop hunts) and breaks of structure. 4) Location: 60-min premium/discount, nearest support and resistance, room to the next level. 5) Momentum and stretch: RSI 7/14/70, MACD histogram, Bollinger z-score. 6) Price action: body strength and wick rejection on the trigger candle. 7) Context filters: session, volatility regime, and news-type spikes.

The model

These 23 measurements feed a regularised logistic model trained on the last ~4,000 one-minute candles, with a 5-minute target. It re-trains itself every 30 minutes. Instead of fixed rules, it learns which factors actually worked recently. For example, in quiet sessions it may learn to fade stretches, and in strong sessions to follow momentum.

Signal grades

A direction is always given. The grade tells you how much to trust it: A+ / STRONG = all filters pass and recent filtered signals beat break-even. MODERATE = filters pass but the recent edge is thin. WEAK = a filter failed (low confidence, off-session, or abnormal volatility). Accuracy comes from being selective. Our tests showed the edge sits mostly in London/New York hours (11:00–22:00 PKT) and in high-confidence readings. Asian-session signals were close to random. Raise the minimum confidence for fewer, stronger trades.

Limits

The data comes from Deriv's public feed, which is not your broker's price feed. Closes can differ slightly. The model cannot know about news. Past accuracy does not guarantee future results. Use a fixed small stake, never martingale.
